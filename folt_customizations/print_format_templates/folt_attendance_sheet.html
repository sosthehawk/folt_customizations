{#- FoLT Attendance Sheet -- the W-04A register printed onto paper. This is the sheet that goes into the room. FoLT already carried one: a blank pre-printed form with these exact columns and nothing in them, which every attendee then filled in by hand. That is where the re-keying in this workflow came from -- somebody had to type 57 handwritten names, locations and mobile numbers afterwards, and handwriting is the one thing that cannot be read back (measured against the real accountability pack: 38% on names, 0% on phone numbers, and a wrong digit in a phone number is a payment to a stranger). So the sheet prints the roster already in it. The attendee's job shrinks to signing against their own name, and the office's job shrinks to ticking. Walk-ins still write, which is why the blank continuation rows exist (F-04A-V6: a walk-in gets no separate process). One template, both directions of the loop. While the register is a draft it is a sheet waiting to be filled in: empty Present and Signature cells, blank rows at the bottom. Once verified, the same format prints the record -- who attended, and how each of them acknowledged. DELIBERATELY NOT PRINTED: id_number, gender, is_pwd, photo_consent. The mobile number has to be here because it is the payout destination and the sheet is the attendee's one chance to correct it. An ID number and a disability flag on paper that circulates a room of strangers and is then scanned to a shared drive have no use on the day, and the register already holds both.
