The paper form is a single sheet that has to answer three questions at a glance: what was spent, line by line, against which budget coding; what the float actually gave; and what the balance is. The worked case came back at minus four shillings on a 262,000 float, so the balance line is spelled out in both directions -- money owed to the staff member reads differently from money they have to return, and the form is where that is settled.
